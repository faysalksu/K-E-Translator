Korean → English

Screen Translator

The floating K→E button is available on webpages. Drag it anywhere, click to freeze and translate the visible screen, then press ESC to release.
Image OCR settings

OCR.space recognizes Korean text and its screen position. Your key is saved locally in this Chrome profile and used only for OCR requests. Do not share it or publish the extension with your key.

Translation uses MyMemory. Screenshot and recognized text are sent to third-party services.
